# Bizele Product

Bizele connects:

1. Social media creators looking for paid sponsorships.
2. Businesses/brands looking for creators and social media advertising.

## Brand side

- AI-powered business insights
- Business performance understanding
- Growth opportunity discovery
- Problem identification
- AI recommendations
- Creator discovery
- Influencer campaign management
- Campaign performance tracking
- Data-driven growth decisions

## Creator side

- Professional creator profile
- Portfolio
- Social account showcase
- Brand discovery
- Campaign discovery
- AI-powered brand/creator matching
- Collaboration management
- Creator career growth

## Current product stage

Pre-launch.

## Current website objective

Build a dynamic waitlist website.

The website is NOT the complete Bizele platform.

Do not implement:
- Creator dashboards
- Brand dashboards
- Campaign management
- AI recommendations
- Creator matching
- Analytics dashboards

Those belong to future product phases.
