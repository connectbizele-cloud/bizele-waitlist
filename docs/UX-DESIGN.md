# UX Design

## Overall direction

Bizele should feel like a premium modern SaaS platform combining:

- Creator economy
- AI
- Marketing intelligence
- Business growth

## Landing page

### Section 1 — Hero

Headline:
Connect Brands With Influence.

Subheadline:
Bizele helps brands discover creators and helps creators discover meaningful paid collaborations.

Primary CTA:
Join the Waitlist

Secondary CTA:
I'm a Creator
I'm a Brand

### Section 2 — The problem

Explain the disconnect between brands and creators.

### Section 3 — Two-sided platform

Creator experience vs Brand experience.

### Section 4 — AI-powered ecosystem

Show how AI assists discovery, matching and growth.

### Section 5 — How Bizele works

Brand → Discover → Match → Campaign → Analyze

Creator → Profile → Discover → Collaborate → Grow

### Section 6 — Waitlist CTA

Dynamic waitlist count.

### Section 7 — Footer

Brand information + social links.
